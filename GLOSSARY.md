# Reader

Language established in the product discussion. Add terms when their meaning is settled.

## Language

**Dictionary**:
An imported collection of words and their definitions or translations, used for lookup while reading.
_Avoid_: Vocabulary list, book glossary

**Monolingual dictionary**:
A dictionary whose definitions are in the same language as its words.

**Bilingual dictionary**:
A dictionary whose entries translate words from one language into another.

**Word lookup**:
Consulting a dictionary, Wikipedia, or web search for a word selected in a book.

**Reading position**:
The device-local place in a book where the reader resumes reading.
_Avoid_: Progress percentage

**Endnote**:
A note or commentary placed at the end of a chapter or book and referenced from another passage.

**Book note**:
A footnote, endnote, or commentary supplied by the book and referenced from its text.
_Avoid_: Annotation note

**Annotation note**:
A note written by the reader and attached to a selected passage.
_Avoid_: Book note

**Highlight**:
A passage marked by the reader for later reference.

**Bookmark**:
A saved location in a book that the reader can return to.

**Note overlay**:
A view of a book's note over the current reading page, leaving the underlying reading position unchanged.
_Avoid_: Jump to note

**Imported book**:
A book copied into the reader's library independently of its original file.

**Library entry**:
The record for an imported book, shared across repeated imports of the exact same EPUB. Different files or editions have separate entries even when their titles match.

**Downloaded book**:
A library book whose file is stored on the device and available for offline reading. A book listed from the server is not necessarily downloaded.

**Remove download**:
An action that removes a book's local file while keeping its entry and server copy available.
_Avoid_: Delete from library

**Delete from library**:
An action that removes a book from the active library across synced devices and places it in trash.
_Avoid_: Remove download

**Trash**:
Deleted books and their annotations retained for 30 days, available for restore or permanent deletion.

**Slider jump**:
A move to another passage using the bottom progress slider. Only slider jumps contribute to reading back/forward history.
